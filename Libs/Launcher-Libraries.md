# Bundled launcher libraries

LibDataBroker-1.1 (minor 4) and LibDBIcon-1.0 (minor 56) are unmodified files
from the official [LibDBIcon v12.0.3 package](https://www.wowace.com/projects/libdbicon-1-0/files/8448436),
matching the copies already used in Avid Angler. Existing embedded LibStub and
CallbackHandler provide their dependencies. No separate installation is needed.

LibDBIcon retains its upstream BSD-style notice in `LICENSE-LibDBIcon.txt`.
LibDataBroker is by Tekkub and contributors, distributed as the dependency in
that official bundle; no relicensing is asserted. See its
[upstream repository](https://github.com/tekkub/libdatabroker-1-1) and
[project notice](https://www.wowace.com/project/20113/license).
