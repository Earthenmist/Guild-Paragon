# 1.8.0-Release

## Changes
- Roster: class icons now appear before member names, alongside class colours, so classes are easier to tell apart at a glance (including for colourblind players). Toggle under Settings > General > Roster Display.
- Roster: new optional "Group online members first" setting lists online members under their own heading above offline members, keeping your chosen sort in each section. Click a heading to hide or show that section.
- Roster: type a class name (for example "priest") in the search box to list only that class.
- Added a Discord icon to Settings > General; it shows the Earthenmist community invite ready to copy.

## Fixes
- Sorting by Last Online no longer mixes online members with members who were online less than an hour ago.

## Known issues
- None currently identified.
