# 1.10.0-Release

## Changes
- Roster: online members who are away now show "AFK" in orange in the Last Online column and member details, instead of green "Online".
- Companion: the Guild Master can now tick "Officers can manage Companion" so officers can change Companion settings and prepare and apply notes, including auto-apply when they log in. Only the Guild Master can change this option or disable Companion. It is off by default.
