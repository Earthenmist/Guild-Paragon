# 1.11.0-Release

## Changes
- Guild masters can now stop Guild Paragon marking new and returning members as Main automatically. Turn on **Don't mark new members as Main** under Settings > Health > New Member Follow-up. New members then stay untagged until an officer tags them, and Guild Health lists them for follow-up. It is off by default and applies to the whole guild. Officers pass it on, so members get it even while the guild master is offline.

## Fixes
- None.

## Known issues
- Members on versions before 1.11.0 still mark new members as Main until they update.
