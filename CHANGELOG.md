# 1.9.2-Release

## Changes
- None.

## Fixes
- Recruitment: recruits waiting for a welcome now appear in Pending Welcomes as soon as they join, instead of only after a reload. The tab also refreshes its other details whenever you open it, and the Pending Welcomes button shows how many are waiting.
- Scrollbars no longer have a bright outline that made them look selected.

## Known issues
- The login summary starts tracking from your first login with 1.9.0 or later, so the first login shows no guild changes.
